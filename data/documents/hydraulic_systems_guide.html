Fluid Specification

ISO VG 46 anti-wear hydraulic oil, cleanliness target ISO 4406 code 18/16/13. Never mix viscosities or brands without flushing.

Daily Checks

  1. Reservoir level at temperature line
  2. Listen for cavitation at pump inlet
  3. Scan for external leaks
  4. Check accumulator pre-charge monthly (nitrogen)

Oil Analysis

Quarterly samples. Elevated silicon indicates dirt ingress; iron indicates pump or valve wear. Water >500 ppm risks cavitation and corrosion.

Common Failures

  • Pump wear: increasing noise, flow loss, case drain flow up
  • Valve sticking: slow cycles, heat at relief valve
  • Cylinder seal bypass: drift under load, oil on rod
  • Contamination: valve spool scoring, filter clogging

Preventive Tasks

Change return filters at 3 bar differential or 2,000 hours. Replace suction strainer at major overhaul only. Hose replacement every 5 years or per manufacturer.

Safety

Depressurize before disconnecting lines. Fluid injection injuries are medical emergencies. Use cardboard for leak search, not hands.
