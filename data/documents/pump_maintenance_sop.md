# Pump-3 Predictive Maintenance SOP

## Overview
Pump-3 is a centrifugal process pump in Building A. This SOP covers vibration monitoring, temperature thresholds, and maintenance scheduling for predictive maintenance decision support.

## Vibration Thresholds
Normal operating vibration: below 4.5 mm/s RMS on the drive-end bearing.
Warning level: 4.5–7.0 mm/s RMS — schedule inspection within 72 hours.
Critical level: above 7.0 mm/s RMS — reduce load immediately and plan bearing replacement within 24 hours.

Elevated vibration combined with rising bearing temperature often indicates imminent bearing failure. Cross-check both sensors before deferring maintenance.

## Temperature Thresholds
Bearing housing temperature normal range: 45–65°C during steady operation.
Warning: 65–80°C — inspect lubrication and alignment.
Critical: above 80°C — stop pump if process allows; verify cooling and bearing condition.

## Recommended Inspection Intervals
- Visual inspection: weekly
- Vibration analysis: monthly
- Oil sample analysis: quarterly
- Bearing replacement: every 18 months or when vibration exceeds warning for 7 consecutive days

## Failure Risk Indicators
High failure risk within 7 days when ALL of the following are true:
1. Vibration above 6.5 mm/s for 3+ consecutive readings
2. Bearing temperature above 75°C
3. Hours since last service exceeds 800

## Maintenance Actions
| Risk Level | Action |
|------------|--------|
| Low | Continue monitoring; log readings in CMMS |
| Medium | Schedule inspection; check alignment and lubrication |
| High | Plan shutdown window; order replacement bearings (Part #BRG-P3-220) |

## Safety
Always lock out / tag out (LOTO) before physical inspection. Never open the coupling guard while the pump is running.
