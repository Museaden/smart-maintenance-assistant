Transformer Maintenance Guide (T-01 through T-06)

1. Overview

This guide covers oil-filled and dry-type distribution transformers serving Production and Utilities. Predictive maintenance focuses on oil quality, thermal performance, and electrical testing.

2. Oil-Filled Transformers (T-01, T-02, T-03)

2.1 Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA)

Sample annually; quarterly for transformers >5 MVA or >20 years age. Key gases:

  • Hydrogen (H2): partial discharge or corona — investigate if >100 ppm
  • Acetylene (C2H2): arcing — immediate investigation if >5 ppm
  • Ethylene (C2H4): oil overheating — trend with temperature

2.2 Oil Physical Tests

TestLimitAction
Dielectric strength>30 kVFilter or replace oil
Moisture<20 ppmDry-out procedure
Acidity<0.15 mg KOH/gOil reclamation

2.3 Visual Inspection (Quarterly)

  • Check silica gel breather — replace when 60% discolored
  • Inspect bushings for cracks or tracking
  • Listen for unusual hum (loose laminations)
  • Verify cooling fans and pumps operate

3. Dry-Type Transformers (T-04, T-05, T-06)

Thermography monthly under load. Hot spot >10°C above similar phase requires internal inspection at outage.

Vacuum clean coils annually. Maintain room ventilation; ambient max 40°C.

4. Electrical Tests (Annual)

  • Insulation power factor (tip-up test)
  • Turns ratio and excitation current
  • DC winding resistance balance

5. Predictive Alerts

Integrate top oil temperature trend with load. Sudden increase at constant load suggests cooling failure or internal fault development.

6. Safety

Assume all bushings energized. Minimum approach distance per voltage class. Only qualified high-voltage technicians perform switching.

7. Spares

Fuses, silica gel, gasket sets on shelf TR-SPARE-KIT. Full transformer replacement lead time 16–24 weeks — maintain contingency plan.
